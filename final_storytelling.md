# Final Storytelling

## Executive answers

- **Do people tell the truth about what they want?** Partially. The declared preferences and the observed evaluations diverge, which suggests social desirability and imperfect self-knowledge.
- **Does attractiveness dominate?** Attractiveness is a strong driver of `YES`, but it is not sufficient alone to explain `MATCH`.
- **Do shared interests matter?** Yes. Interest alignment and compatibility signals are among the most stable drivers of match formation.
- **Does age matter?** Yes, but mostly through age proximity and gap rather than age as an isolated variable.
- **Does race matter?** Some demographic signals can be significant, but they should be interpreted carefully and ethically.
- **Can we predict a match?** Yes, with moderate-to-strong performance depending on feature availability. The realistic model remains informative, while the leaky model inflates performance.

## Actionable recommendations

1. Focus the matching logic on compatibility and shared interests, not only on attractiveness.
2. Use age-gap and preference alignment as high-value matching features.
3. Keep pre-date and in-date variables separated from post-date signals in any predictive use case.
4. Monitor demographic variables carefully to avoid unfair or over-interpreted conclusions.
