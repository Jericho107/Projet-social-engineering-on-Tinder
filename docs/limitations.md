# Limitations

- The dataset is a public speed-dating experiment and is not representative of all populations or relationship contexts.
- Missingness, survey design and participant selection can affect observed patterns.
- Statistical association does not establish causal influence on attraction or matching.
- Model performance depends on the historical experiment setup and should not be treated as a deployable dating-product benchmark.
- Feature-importance or SHAP-style explanations describe model behaviour, not psychological causality.
- The governance layer focuses on leakage and interpretation boundaries rather than production MLOps.
